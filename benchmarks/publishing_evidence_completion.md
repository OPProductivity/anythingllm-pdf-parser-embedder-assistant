# Complete publishing evidence before author adjudication

Author selection now follows a declared acquisition plan and a review of the applicable specialists. A positive early candidate does not cancel the remaining first-five-page native or needed image/weak-text OCR tasks. Front matter and short editorial boundaries can extend acquisition. Every scheduled OCR task is attempted and failures remain in the trace. Byte-identical inputs reuse content-bound evidence; each filename still runs its own inference.

Publishing-role claims distinguish a person, responsibility, occurrence and work. Equivalent author-rights wording, complete local contributor rosters, late editorial signatures, corporate citation/responsibility, interviewer question turns and later running-head corroboration supplement the existing parsers. Regions such as copyright, footer, dedication or abstract are context, not unconditional author vetoes. Explicit personal report bylines outrank an institutional citation of the same report. Publisher ownership does not establish personal authorship. Unfinished OCR tokens and incomplete roster prefixes are deferred.

Twenty purposive first-five-page manual comparisons were recorded before implementation, with two additional regression reviews. The code was compared with the page evidence and actual candidate/decision journeys. The coordinated chapter fixture was corrected to the printed contributor roster order, retaining its coordinator as a separate role. Its test now requires the recovered complete credit instead of requiring abstention.

## Validation and limits

The actual sampled metadata entry path was replayed on all 770 hash-verified copies with GROBID disabled. Planned native/OCR acquisition and inference were exercised; full PDF preparation, upload, embedding, installation and live Desktop behavior were not qualified. The full offline suite passed 3,031 tests, with one skipped and 34 deselected; 28 subtests passed. Ruff, core Pyright and the tracked-file secret check passed. New-module formatting was followed by focused architecture regression checks.

This fuller acquisition performs more OCR than early-success selection. The benchmark partitions and production baseline ran with different parallel execution, so recorded times are not a controlled latency comparison. Content caching avoids repeating OCR solely because a filename changes. Further efficiency work must preserve the evidence plan.

Praise/endorsement subject-speaker semantics, general parent-work inheritance, edition identity, image-only branding, calibrated probabilities and some genre boundaries remain incomplete. Scores are heuristic priorities. Shared clues are allowed; the development result does not establish independent statistical significance or transfer to an untouched corpus.
