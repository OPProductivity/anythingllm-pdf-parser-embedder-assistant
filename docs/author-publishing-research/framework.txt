Publishing responsibility framework: complete evidence and contextual priority
17 October 2026

Authorship is responsibility for a particular work or contribution. An occurrence of a name is evidence about that relation, not the relation itself. Recognition must distinguish the agent, role, work, edition and evidence occurrence. A title page, a source-platform wrapper, a copyright page, a review and a bibliography can name the same person for different reasons. Conversely, the same person may legitimately hold several roles. The implementation seeks the credited responsible creator of the selected bibliographic unit; it cannot infer undisclosed real-world contributions from typography.

Evidence acquisition precedes verdict. Every request independently acquires its planned native pages and relevant image regions, including when an early expert already proposes an author. Native text, marker-cleaned baselines, raw positioned spans and OCR alternatives remain complementary views. No reusable PDF/OCR evidence cache is used across requests. Sharing the transient bundle within one request avoids redundant acquisition without hiding later evidence. A positive guess never cancels scheduled OCR or closing-page inspection.

The opening plan always contains the available first five PDF pages. Publishing structure can extend it through page twelve. A copyright/publisher word inside ordinary body prose or the closing pages is not evidence that the opening remains front matter. Later body boundaries close an opening apparatus sequence without depending on an author guess. If contents, preface or other opening apparatus still continues at that boundary, the collector follows it in small batches through a declared maximum opening window of thirty-two pages. The limit is recorded when reached. The final two pages and short editorial signature scope are separate acquisition extensions. This is bounded opening/closing metadata acquisition, not a claim to read an entire book. The opening plan is passed to responsibility, title, rights and field adapters so acquisition is not defeated by a second fixed twelve-page cutoff. Closing pages are not silently reclassified as title pages.

Initial metadata sampling reads all available first five pages and records page-local failures without cancelling later reads. A sampling warning no longer prevents author inference. A failed native page still receives a rendered-image attempt when rendering is possible. Acquisition failures remain visible. Empty OCR, subprocess failure and timeouts are different from finding no responsible creator. Dark cover inversion, tight-line borders, quality-driven orientation retries, facing-leaf crops and retained alternative readings address specific observed weaknesses. A successful OCR command is not proof of a correct name. The current local OCR language installation and difficult scripts remain practical limits; no universal multilingual OCR claim follows from English raster controls. Small text-image bands remain intact instead of being split as facing leaves; their OCR coordinates can be combined with native title/affiliation geometry. Clean sparse native text without an image or vector artwork does not need an artificial OCR pass. Genuine full-page images with predominantly invisible OCR text receive a new reading: searchable text is a fallible transcription, not ground truth. Visible native bylines and high-confidence direct rereads of invisible bylines have different contextual priorities, with their reasons recorded. A visible native credit is protected from a disagreeing OCR reread. The native and OCR paths must preserve the difference between a source that contains a credit and an excerpt that omits it.

Experts may use the same visual, textual and OCR cues. Correlation is permitted. Their contribution depends on what their expertise establishes in the present context. A footer detector that knows only a location has little reason to overturn a title-linked author. A footnote expert that establishes a cited title, marker linkage and a different work has a stronger reason to reject that occurrence as the selected work's byline. Neither conclusion globally invalidates the person. A separate authorship occurrence can remain decisive. Heuristic priorities are ordering values, not calibrated probabilities, and are not compared as if an 80-percent region label and a 70-percent author label concerned the same event.

Responsibility assertions are collected before selection. Structured fields, rights clauses, chapter credits, native article rosters, publisher title pages and acquired OCR regions enumerate their interpretations. Compatibility entry points may return one report to older callers only after their report generators are consumed. The historical selector remains one expert; its result is open to challenge. Explicit unresolved conflicts of comparable same-work responsibility produce an unresolved verdict with both claims retained, not first-wins selection. Some legacy compatibility heuristics remain bounded and specialized; this is not a claim that every historical helper has become a universal document parser.

Sparse title-page structure can propose alternative work titles when embedded Title is absent or not visible. Title and author can have equal font sizes when a responsibility label establishes their boundary; short two-word titles remain legitimate. Every resulting title/genre interpretation is assessed by the same specialists, without treating the nearby name as an author merely because the title hypothesis exists.

A complete roster is a publishing unit. Several authors may share one institution, affiliations may occupy multiple lines, and names may wrap across lines. Eight lines, six author-affiliation pairs and one affiliation line are not publishing laws. Contiguous name rows and title-bound name/email blocks are inspected through their actual body/affiliation boundary. A later complete physical roster can challenge an accepted partial result. Byline rows can extend left of a centered title, three separated author columns retain three people, and removed superscript markers must not glue a surname to the word and. Dates are publication fields, not roster members; they may separate a full byline from its abstract even when affiliations are relocated to footnotes. Small contact typography does not split names and same-baseline email columns. Geometric suffixes remain recorded partial interpretations rather than competing complete rosters. Editorial sidebars cannot interrupt the main title column or transfer their named editors into the author band. numbered affiliations can use acronyms and the same font size as the names. A preceding author/affiliation pair prevents treating only its later pair as the entire roster. Native and OCR specialists are admitted by these structures even when an initial genre guess says web article or chapter. Correspondence, equal-contribution stars, editor labels and reviewer handles have distinct functions. An email below the body boundary or in a praise/contact panel is not an opening-author roster. A group of collaborators is not automatically an author group; the applicable source statement must establish that responsibility.

Work binding must be local enough to separate related publications. In a series advertisement, a title's By attribution closes its relationship before the next book's Edited by clause. A distant title match cannot transfer those editors to the selected book. Body/field delimiters replace character windows that could cut a catalogue editor name in half. Complete Name - Title filename credits can identify an article as well as an excerpt when the acquired native or OCR title corroborates the selected work. On a title page, blank lines around AND do not terminate a joint editorial roster. On an article wrapper, a broad repository Author(s) field may include a translator; revision requires an independently read local byline and a separate translation credit explaining the extra person. A translator label alone is not a veto because a person can also be an author.

Copyright ownership, attribution rights, bibliographic creation and production contribution remain distinct. An institutional owner is not thereby the personal writer. A title-bound personal copyright roster may corroborate a separately established full byline. Rights wording varies in voice, order, pluralization and role vocabulary; the catalogue explores these families rather than asserting that a single English sentence is the rule. Foreword signatures, dedication names, endorsements, book-review headings, indexes and captions need their own work targets. Praise speaker and praised subject are different agents; a surname in an endorsement does not justify inventing a full given name.

Genre is reconsiderable when newly acquired structure supplies a reason. It must not switch merely because another strategy happens to return a convenient name. Book/chapter/issue boundaries are independent of page count. A short book can have an editorial title page; a long selected chapter is still a contribution. Institutional reports, legal opinions, news agencies, teaching handouts and signed editorials can have responsibility conventions unlike an academic abstract-and-byline article. Typed corporate and surname-only credits preserve those conventions without forcing every result through a Western two-word personal-name model.

Manual evidence comes before code claims. The local review contains a uniform first-five-page visual and literal-code record for every historical content item, plus separately recorded fresh-work reviews. The historical 770 filename cases are 383 byte-distinct inputs, including one HTML payload disguised as PDF; they are not 770 independent PDFs. Related scans and excerpts also share parent works. Canonical creator names and source-printed name forms remain distinguishable, and parent-work attribution is documented when the first five pages omit a credit. A one-page divider, a whole issue and an excluded assignment retain explicit scope notes. Ordinary extraction failures are not labelled exceptional merely to improve the denominator.

The research catalogue contains 500 proposed templates across 25 categories, twenty per category, with 122 separate recorded occurrences and a growing primary-source register. All 500 individual trials are executed and their failures retained: supplied-text semantic controls are distinct from actual generated-PDF layout/OCR controls. Passing a synthetic template does not prove corpus correctness, and observing a wording does not make it a universal rule. Twenty is an exploration minimum, not statistical representativeness. The complete private workbook, source images, downloads and per-case traces remain local; public Git contains general mechanisms, invented counterexamples and aggregate qualification only.

Embedded PDF Author remains diagnostic-only; retained metadata can still help through Title and work identity. Qualification reports original filenames, neutral filenames with embedded metadata retained, and pure neutral filenames with active Info/XMP removed separately. Pure copies are reopened to verify empty active metadata, matching page counts and identical first-five native text and raster pixels. This is not a claim of forensic removal of unreferenced historical PDF objects or full-document visual equivalence. An earlier stripping attempt left active Info metadata in some copies; those pure-neutral scores are invalidated and replaced, not used as a baseline. Metadata inference is distinct from full preparation, upload, embedding and installed Desktop operation.

Canonical complete-roster accuracy, source-supported name forms, initials, surname-only results, corporate creators, correct whole-issue abstentions, incomplete rosters and wrong nonempty values are reported separately. A two-word surname is still surname-only. Paired filename/metadata dependence and manually grouped work families describe failure mechanisms without claiming statistical independence. Development-reviewed and fresh-work results remain separate. Once a fresh sample informs a refinement it becomes development evidence for that revision; a later frozen revision receives a separately downloaded unseen-work set. Original and neutral results are not hidden inside a combined score. The local first-request timing comparison must remain at or below the requested 1.7 ratio; operating-system file caching is not a reusable recognition-evidence cache.

A dated first-person personal weblog masthead supplies a recorded site-owner hypothesis. It does not identify the writer of a particular unsigned post. A public real-PDF control exposed that unsupported transfer; the hypothesis now remains tied to the hosting site and cannot fill or overwrite the post byline. Explicit guest-post labels prevent even that association.

Remaining research includes broader praise-subject relations, reference-entry boundaries, uncertain or pseudonymous attributions, broader group responsibility, multilingual OCR and name morphology, versioned name changes, missing parent-work evidence, and eventual calibration on independently grouped data. These are ordinary publishing possibilities, not reasons to encode source-name exceptions or treat the present corpus as the universe of publishing. The long-term near-760/770 objective remains distinct from any currently measured candidate score. GROBID is not used.

An ORCID icon is an identifier marker when its own link geometry establishes that role; the same letters in a real surname remain name text. Wide gaps and affiliation indices can delimit people without commas. A locally explicit personal byline representing a named consortium can credit both personal and collective responsibility without expanding a membership list. Back-matter acknowledgments following an established abstract/body boundary do not reopen the front matter. These decisions preserve the evidence while constraining its relationship to the selected work.

Feature acquisition has two purposes: learning how publishing works and deciding responsibility for one new input at an acceptable cost. Discovery may spend more to expose missed rooms. The same experts assess broad and production evidence; manual truth remains independent of both. Acquisition is only one failure layer: reaching a clue, reading it, interpreting its work/role, admitting a proposal and selecting a verdict are recorded separately. Broad inspection can corroborate, rescue or confuse a verdict. Current confidence must not be the only criterion for selecting lab cases; confident successes, ordinary failures and independent exploratory cases all require challenges. The lab can read beyond structures the present planner already knows.

Computation can be optimized without narrowing evidence. In the native roster expert, prefix facts about earlier author/affiliation pairs and role labels are computed once. Recognition of independent materialized images can overlap while PDF access stays on its calling thread. All scheduled tasks finish before adjudication, failures remain visible, and image/feature state is not retained for a subsequent input. Broad laboratory records are research artifacts rather than a production recognition cache.

A discovered chapter boundary must survive final adjudication. Parent-volume editors remain parent-volume evidence when a local chapter credit and subsequent author heads establish a selected contribution. On an edition title page, a physically separate primary-creator block can establish a different role from a later editor or translator credit. Display-list grammar preserves personal suffixes and internal surname hyphens while recognizing middle-dot separators. Publisher logos and catalogue physical-description fields close responsibility spans rather than extending surnames. A visible complete role-bound roster can explain a same-page truncated OCR reading; a genuinely different complete roster remains competing evidence.

Production computation can also avoid unused raster encodings: prepare the primary image first and render another scale only if a scheduled OCR retry requests it. The same PDF-owner thread handles those requests, with recognition confined to independent image bytes. This changes when an image is encoded, not which planned evidence or retry may be acquired. Repeated contextual-role scans parse invariant candidate rosters once per page while still assessing every region.

A hidden transcription can have the form of a plausible personal byline while its visible image spells the name differently. A small, directly observed credit reread may then add more useful information than further broad pages. Image alternative text can share an extraction line with much smaller byline spans; physical baseline and size relations recover a candidate crop without deleting the alternative text or stopping full-page acquisition. A separate labelled interpretation binds each eligible visible reread back to that exact native line. Original native text, full-page OCR and conflicting alternate rereads remain available. Production limits this additional crop to person-shaped explicit credits; the lab may explore ordinary By spans as well. An unfinished roster is never promoted as complete.
