Publishing responsibility framework: research expansion, 17 October 2026

Authorship is a relation between an agent, a contribution, and a particular work. A page is a surface containing many such relations. The task is to reconstruct the relevant work and responsibility claims, not select the most person-shaped text in the most familiar position.

The catalogue contains 500 proposed normalized templates across 25 categories, twenty per category, plus 30 separately recorded observations. Twenty is a practical exploration minimum, not evidence of statistical representativeness. Several templates share a semantic family. They are not 500 independent discoveries or production rules. Twenty-four source records and seven downloaded PDFs broaden the conventions studied; this is a bounded research pass, not an exhaustive survey of world publishing. All exact proposed templates still need trials. Sources explain conventions; their IDs do not certify every proposed sentence as an observed quotation.

A useful grammar has slots for agent, act, role, object/work, edition, location, marker linkage, and evidence span. Surface variation includes voice (active/passive), role labels, punctuation, line wrapping, order, plural rosters, initials, scripts, degrees and omitted headings. A missing Abstract label does not erase the relationship between a title, complete byline, affiliations and opening prose. Conversely, a large two-word line is not a byline solely because its typography resembles one.

Legal authorship, bibliographic authorship, copyright ownership and production contribution remain distinct. A work-for-hire statute can assign legal authorship to an employer; the software must still represent the printed personal contribution and chosen metadata policy. No code should silently resolve that policy by treating ownership as authorship. The new textbook inspected here explicitly separates senior personal contributors, institutional ownership and donor lists. That provides a practical negative example against owner-only and name-roster-only rules.

Region labels and responsibility assertions are compatible propositions. The correct question is not whether an 80% footnote label exceeds a 70% author label: those labels concern different events. A footnote specialist contributes most when it identifies the note's function and target, such as an external citation, affiliation, funding disclosure, translator credit or author correspondence. A low-page location alone carries little authority against a well-linked byline. A citation pointing to another named work provides stronger reason to reject that particular occurrence as a current-work byline. If a separate current-work authorship occurrence exists, it remains valid. The twenty contextual priority trials record these distinctions.

Experts may share native text, visual geometry and OCR. Correlation is allowed. A repeated reading is not forbidden evidence, and an expert with additional role information can change its contribution even on the same page. The software's integer priorities are currently heuristic ordering values, not calibrated probability estimates. Counting several outputs from one input as independent confirmations would give a misleading explanation; explaining each output's added relation is the requirement. Calibration, if introduced later, must be learned on manually verified documents and checked on document-grouped holdouts. Neither a high percentage nor a small p-value follows from development-set score gains.

Acquisition is a commitment made before verdict: examine planned native pages, perform needed image-region/scan OCR, record every attempt, then adjudicate all acquired clues. A positive guess cannot shorten that plan. OCR can return empty, time out or merge tokens. Each failure must be recorded. Native geometry can preserve affiliations as separate columns while sorted text can intermingle them. Keep complementary views. Do not claim that a successful OCR command guarantees its transcript is correct or every image has been understood.

Work boundaries can be revised during reading. A scanned facing page may contain the end of one entry and the beginning of another; a whole journal issue contains several contributions; a review embeds the title and author of another book; an institutional report includes sponsor, author and publisher. Genre changes require content reasons in the trace. A parser returning a convenient name is not a reason to switch genre.

Manual transfer phase: eight further source comparisons were recorded before code changes, after the earlier twenty trials and two regression reviews. Four supplied bounded mechanisms for implementation: role-delimited professional byline, native complete byline followed by affiliations and prose without Abstract, and personal copyright roster linked to a separate opening byline. The Frontiers case additionally exposed a transcription boundary: superscript whitespace was being discarded and merged a surname with the conjunction before the final coauthor. That implementation defect is corrected without changing name identities. The other four examples remain future work or deferrals; none receives a name exception.

Qualification separates (1) manual source judgement, (2) manually proposed transfer rules, (3) source-shaped counterexample tests, (4) original and neutral filename metadata replay, and (5) wider-corpus replay. The frozen ground truth is unchanged. Current research PDFs are not added to the 770 denominator. This corpus is development-reviewed, not untouched holdout evidence. 770 filename cases correspond to 383 byte-distinct inputs, not 770 statistically independent works. Related scans/excerpts can share a work and must be grouped in a future blind split.

Implementation gaps remain: complete praise subject/speaker links; native reference-work entry boundaries; general geographic affiliations; robust review/book nesting; editor/translator output-role policy; semantic parsing across all proposed wording families; multilingual name handling; confidence calibration; and retry policy for transient incomplete acquisition caches. Catalogue inclusion is not completion of these capabilities. The target near 760/770 remains a long-term objective, and remaining misses must not all be described as exceptional documents.

Boundary correction after wider replay: the first research candidate produced 8 gains but exposed 7 filename-case regressions across three inputs. Their layouts alternate person, institution, person, institution before an unlabelled abstract. A first institution terminates one person-affiliation relation, not the whole credit apparatus. The corrected contiguous-band expert inspects up to the first prose block for another name at the byline type size followed by an institution; it then defers to complete-roster experts. Smaller affiliation continuations do not qualify as new personal credits. This guards a publishing structure, not the names or IDs of the failing papers. The three source reviews and the failed source snapshot remain available. A 17-case focused replay covers both corrected failures and targeted gains before the final full replay.
